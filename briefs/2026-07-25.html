Confirmed in-window, July 24

Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5, near Fable 5 capability at half the price

Anthropic released Opus 5 on July 24, saying it approaches Fable 5 in many categories at half the price, and even beats Fable 5 on several published benchmarks including agentic coding, computer use, and multidisciplinary reasoning. Pricing holds at 5 dollars per million input tokens and 25 per million output, matching Opus 4.8, and a new effort toggle (low, medium, high) lets users trade cost against capability per task. It is Anthropic's fourth model release in under two months after Mythos 5, Fable 5, and Sonnet 5. Yesterday's brief carried the rollout as [UNVERIFIED]; today it is confirmed, a 1-day early-signal payoff. Source: techcrunch.com, fortune.com

OpenAI adds voice control for computer use and multi-agent coordination in ChatGPT Work and Codex

OpenAI shipped voice control for computer use and multi-agent coordination in ChatGPT Work and Codex on July 24. Users can direct their computer and coordinate multiple agents by speaking, powered by GPT-Live, which listens, talks, and drives work in the app simultaneously. This lands a day after Codex engineer Tibo Sottiaux teased a July 24 reveal. Source: releasebot.io, openai.com

ChatGPT gets a Health experience for eligible US users

OpenAI launched a ChatGPT Health experience for eligible US users on web and iOS, connecting supported health records and Apple Health data into a dashboard with lab results, medications, activity, and sleep, plus question answering and trend exploration on top. It is OpenAI's most direct move yet into regulated personal health data. Source: releasebot.io

Nvidia invests 1 billion dollars in Naver's Korean AI data center

Nvidia announced on July 24 it will invest 1 billion dollars in Naver to help finance an AI data center under construction in South Korea, developed jointly with Brookfield. The funding lets Naver more than triple the facility's size, and the site will run on Nvidia AI hardware. It extends Nvidia's pattern of financing its own demand across Korea after the SK Group accord. Source: bloomberg.com

Nvidia ships Cosmos 3 Edge and MCP hooks into creative and simulation tools

Nvidia released Cosmos 3 Edge, a 4 billion parameter world model optimized for on-device physical AI and robotics, and detailed integrations that let AI agents drive creative and simulation tools directly over the Model Context Protocol. The MCP angle matters: simulation and content pipelines join the growing list of surfaces agents can operate natively. Source: aiagentstore.ai

Paper raises a 34 million dollar Series A for design tooling aimed at AI coding agents

Paper, a San Francisco startup building a design platform for teams shipping software with AI coding agents, closed a 34 million dollar Series A led by Accel and Iconiq, with engineers and designers from Anthropic and OpenAI joining as angels. A bet that the agent-written-software wave needs its own design layer. Source: aiweekly.co
